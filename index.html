Henry Arthur

M.Sc. Student, Cognitive Science — University of Trento (CIMeC) & Ev's Language Lab, MIT

I am broadly interested in language and reasoning in human and artificial intelligence. How can we measure model-human alignment, and what is required to develop machines that are general solvers? I am also curious how these questions inform the way we regulate and treat AI in our daily lives. I spend as much time as I can climbing rock and ice.

Background

Previously, I spent time doing research at NASA, working on AI tooling to prepare for future Mars missions, and before that at Fermilab on the Mu2e experiment.
